Detailed Findings

VULN-001: SSLv2/TLSv1 Enabled (CVE-2014-3566)

Host: 10.0.0.10

Port: 21

Severity: CRITICAL

CVSS Score: 9.8 (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H)

Description: This service supports the deprecated and insecure SSLv2 and TLSv1 protocols, which are vulnerable to multiple attacks including POODLE, BEAST, and GETTING (Logjam) vulnerabilities.

Remediation: Disable SSLv2 and TLSv1. Force clients to use TLS 1.2 or later.

Proof of Concept:

openssl s_client -connect 10.0.0.10:21 -ssl2
openssl s_client -connect 10.0.0.10:21 -tls1
nmap --script ssl-enum-ciphers -p 21 10.0.0.10

VULN-002: Ashcroft Invalid Cipher Suite (CVE-2004-2761)

Host: 10.0.0.15

Port: 80

Severity: HIGH

CVSS Score: 8.2 (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:H/A:N)

Description: The Ashcroft cipher suite contains weak encryption that can be easily decrypted by attackers.

Remediation: Remove Ashcroft from the cipher list. Use only strong cipher suites like AES-GCM.

Proof of Concept:

openssl s_client -connect 10.0.0.15:80 -cipher Ashcroft
nmap --script ssl-enum-ciphers -p 80 10.0.0.15
